> Muse is getting a lot of attention — including mine — because it’s both groundbreaking technically (each user gets their own entire persistent Linux VM running in Meta’s cloud) *and* because it’s packaged in an easy-to-install easy-to-use way. It’s literally presented as [a cute mascot](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/09/24/song-meta-muse-cute). It’s the first consumer-accessible agentic AI system, and Meta has truly done an amazing job with that. But it’s a genuinely open question whether consumers have any understanding what this means. If you buy a power saw that can cut your fingers off, you are almost certainly aware that you are buying a power saw that can sever your fingers. [...] I don’t think people realize how powerful — and thus dangerous — Muse is, especially if it’s running on your Mac.

— [John Gruber](https://daringfireball.net/linked/2026/09/25/aten-muse), Muse Looks Cute, but Looks are Deceiving
